WELCOME

Netball for everyone.

Antwerp Netball Club is a social and empowering club for people who want to play netball, get active and meet new people.

Whether you are completely new to netball or already have experience, you are welcome. We are building a friendly community where everyone can learn, play and enjoy the game.
