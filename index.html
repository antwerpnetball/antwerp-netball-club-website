DISCOVER NETBALL

What is netball?

Netball is a fast-paced ball sport played between two teams of seven players on court. Teams work to keep or regain possession, move the ball into the goal circle and score as many goals as possible.

⏱

3-second passing

Once you receive the ball, you have three seconds to pass or shoot. Quick decisions keep the game moving.

FAST TEAMWORK
◯

Strict non-contact

Players defend without physical contact, so timing, footwork and anticipation are essential.

SMART DEFENCE
▦

Dedicated court areas

Each position can move only in specific thirds of the court, giving every role a clear tactical job.

TACTICAL ROLES
⚑

Precision scoring

Only the Goal Shooter and Goal Attack can score, making shooting accuracy and smart movement crucial.

SCORING EXCITEMENT

NEW TO NETBALL?

Think korfbal meets basketball

If you know korfbal or basketball, you already have a good starting point for understanding netball.

Like korfbal

Passing, movement, teamwork and specific court areas.

Like basketball

A raised goal, scoring, attack and defence.

But netball is different

No dribbling, a three-second rule, seven positions and strict movement areas.

A SHORT STORY

How netball grew

Netball developed from early forms of women's basketball in the late 19th century. As the game evolved, it developed its own rules, positions and style of play.

1890s

Early forms of women's basketball develop in England.

NETBALL AROUND THE WORLD

A global game, still growing

Netball has strong communities in countries including Australia, New Zealand, England, Jamaica, South Africa, Uganda, Malawi, Singapore, Scotland and Wales, among many others.

The sport is also growing across Europe, including in the Netherlands, France and Germany.

In Belgium, netball is still relatively new — and Antwerp Netball Club is helping introduce the game and build a local netball community in Antwerp.

Games & Tournaments

Antwerp Netball Club participates in friendly games throughout the season and in the Tri-Country Cup (TTC).

The TTC is a small league-style cup competition between clubs from Belgium, the Netherlands and Germany. It has 5 Cup Days, with each participating club hosting one Cup Day, followed by a Finals Day.
